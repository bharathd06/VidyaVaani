SDG 4 — Quality Education
For Every Student

VidyaVaani is built on the principle that every student, regardless of language or location, deserves access to world-class education. Using cutting-edge AI, we break the language barrier that locks millions out of quality learning.

🎯 Multilingual Access 🤖 AI-Powered Learning 🏫 Remote Education 📱 Digital Inclusion 🌐 5 Indian Languages

🔗 How VidyaVaani Advances SDG 4

🎤

AI-Powered Language Access

Our IndicTrans2 translation engine and faster-whisper transcription make English lectures instantly accessible in Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and Malayalam — covering over 600 million speakers.

🏫

Offline-Ready for Remote Schools

Built with locally deployed Mistral LLMs, VidyaVaani works without relying on cloud APIs — making it deployable in rural schools with limited or no internet connectivity.

📊

Personalized & Inclusive Pedagogy

Auto-generated smart notes, comprehension quizzes, and an AI tutor ensure every student can learn at their own pace and revisit concepts in their mother tongue.

🔬

Innovation in Educational Infrastructure

Combining speech recognition, neural TTS translation, and domain-specific glossaries (CS, Physics, Biology, Maths), we build a robust, scalable academic knowledge pipeline.
